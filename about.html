The Story So Far

From a fry box with attitude to a regular spot at Hyperdome.

Landing the pitch

We called our shot at Hyperdome

Every good fighter picks their ring, and we picked ours: outside, under the big screen, right across from Hyperdome Plaza, with Event Cinemas at our back. Good food, great vibes, epic spot.

We stepped in the ring Thursday 3 September, and we've been trading Thursdays, Fridays and Saturdays ever since.

It's official

The fighter behind the fryer

The deal got made the way every good deal should — signed at the table, belt on display, no doubt left in the room. Ko's Loaded Fries was coming to Hyperdome, full stop.

The team behind Ko's built this brand on one idea: fries and burgers loud enough, loaded enough, and bold enough to actually knock your socks off. No half-measures.
